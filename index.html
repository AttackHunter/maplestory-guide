What you are actually playing

The cap is level 100 and 3rd job is not in at launch, so Hermit, Chief Bandit and I/L Mage do not exist yet. Everything here is 1st job, 2nd job, and 70 to 100 as a 2nd-jobber.

What breaks old guides
  • Skill max levels changed. Nimble Body, Keen Eyes, Magic Guard and Max MP Increase cap at 15. Old v62 or v83 SP builds do not add up.
  • Haste is weaker: +30 speed, +10 jump.
  • Crit Rate and Crit Damage are real stats on gear and skills.
  • Scrolls: Lesser (100%), Intermediate (60%), Greater (10%), Chaos.
  • Shields have a Guard %. All three builds should wear one.
  • Forgotten Hollow is not open yet. Skip any guide map named Primeval Forest, Forgotten Entrance or Perilous Descent.
  • No stat requirement for 1st job. Death costs EXP. An SP Reset Scroll costs 7,000 NX (about $7) in the Cash Shop.

Which one to start with

Start with the Bandit. Best single-target damage, no ammo to buy, and its mesos pay for the Assassin's stars and the mage's MP potions. Play the I/L second if you want the fastest leveling: Thunder Bolt hits 6 mobs from level 41.

Kerning City Party Quest

4 players, level 21 and up, 30 minutes for the whole run. No level cap and no job limit. The party leader talks to Lakelis by the sewer entrance in Kerning City. Several parties can run at the same time.

Is it worth it? Run it for the gear, not the EXP.
  • Squishy Shoes (a chance when King Slime dies): level 28, any class, no stat requirement, +1 to all four stats, 18 weapon defense, 7 magic defense, 5 slots. Tradeable.
  • Companion's Magic Box for every member at the end: ores, potions, scrolls or gear.
Before your first run

Take Proof of Companionship from Lakelis. Kill King Slime once for 1,870 EXP, 687 mesos and a random Intermediate earring scroll (STR, DEX, INT or LUK). One time only.

Stage 1: Cloto's questions

22 Ligators drop coupons. Cloto asks every member except the leader a question. Hand Cloto exactly that many coupons to get a pass, and drop the spares.

The August test also showed answers of 21 and 30, so there are more questions. Read Cloto's line before collecting.

Drew the 35? Make that player the leader, since the leader is never asked. The old leader then talks to Cloto and draws a fresh question, often a cheaper one.

Stages 2 to 4: the puzzles

Three members stand on three spots, then the leader checks with Cloto. Click a combination once you have tried it so the party never repeats one. Reset before the next run.

Last stage: King Slime

Curse Eyes at the top, Jr. Neckis in the middle, King Slime at the bottom. All 10 drop a coupon, and the leader hands the 10 to Cloto at the top. Jr. Neckis dodge a lot, so make sure someone has the accuracy to hit them.

For your three
  • Bandit: melee means eating his hits. Bring plenty of HP potions.
  • Assassin: the safest of the three here. Stay at range and keep throwing.
  • I/L Wizard: he resists both your elements. Thunder Bolt the Jr. Neckis and Curse Eyes and let the others take the boss.
  • The slam: in the old game, jumping just before he lands let the shockwave pass under you. Not confirmed in Classic yet.

Bonus room and the way out

After the boss comes one wave of 36 mushrooms (24 Horny, 12 Green) in a small room. There are no portals anywhere in the PQ: talk to Nella, who stands on every map, to leave.

If someone dies or drops

  • Dying no longer ends the run. Walk back in.
  • Three players can still finish. Stage 1 needs only 2 passes, and the leader fills the third puzzle spot.
  • Down to two before the puzzles are done: the run is over. Leave and rebuild. Two players already on the boss stage can still finish.
  • A trade-blocked player cannot drop passes. Make that player the leader.

Finding a party

  • Parties form next to Lakelis. Type J>PQ with your level and class to join, or L>PQ / R>PQ if you are building one.
  • Finished your coupons early? Help the slowest member, and pick up stray coupons and passes.
  • Say so before you go AFK, and never leave or kick mid-run.

Good to know

  • Moving Cash Shop items between your characters: leave the item in the Cash Inventory inside the Cash Shop, log into the other character on the same world, and take it out there. Once an item is in a character's own inventory, check its tooltip: "Untradable" means it stays.
  • Pet skills vanish if the pet's 30-day lifespan runs out. Keep a Water of Life ready once you invest in skills.
  • Meso Magnet and Item Pouch are not in the Cash Shop. In the original game they were bought with mesos at the Henesys pet shop. Not confirmed for Classic yet.
  • Crate items can be sold to other players or traded in for Aurora Stamps. If you want one specific item, buying it from a player for mesos can be cheaper than rolling. Halloween and Aurora crates are in the game data but not on sale yet.

Octopus Leg

Maps here are ranked by how fast Octopuses respawn, not just how many there are.

Farm at Caution Falling Down or Northern Top of Construction Site, two hidden streets near Kerning City. Only the Octopus (level 12) drops it. You need 30 for a Kerning City citizenship donation.

Damp Tree-Forest has the most Octopuses, but they come back at under half the rate, so the two hidden streets win. Octopuses also show up in small numbers (2 each) in Swampy Land in a Deep Forest, Hunting Ground in the Deep Forest I and Sleepy Dungeon I; not worth it.

  • Easy kills: 191 HP, 7 avoid. You need 24 accuracy at level 12 to never miss. They walk back and forth on their own floor and give 23 EXP and about 24 mesos each.
  • Take Andre's Request in Kerning City first (level 10+): "defeat 30 Octopus", done while you farm.
  • Bonus drops players have seen: Adamantium Ore (about 199), Aquamarine Ore (about 500), Fragment of Magic, Red Potions, and the Red Thief Hood, a level 15 thief hat.
  • The drop rate is not published. Check your count after the first 50 kills to estimate how long 30 legs will take.

Skip these

    Boss timers

    The game never shows when a boss is up. Two ways to know: Megaphone sightings on each row shows players shouting things like "Zombie Mushmom CH28"; and when you kill a boss (or watch it die), type the channel and press Killed, and the board counts down to when it can come back there.

    BossWhereRespawnYour channelsRecord a kill

    "Killed (min ago)" is for a kill you saw earlier: it applies to the next kill you record. The 1-hour bosses can come back from 60 minutes after the kill, and players see them by about 120, so a card says "can spawn any minute" in between and "should be up" after. Timers stay in this browser. Browsers slow timers in hidden tabs, so a beep can come up to a minute late there.

      After level 70

      Levels 70 to 90 take about 72 million EXP, a bit over twice everything from 1 to 70. Level 90 to 100 is a wall: about 18 billion EXP. Classic uses the old pre-Big Bang EXP table up to level 89, then each level costs 2.1 times the one before; level 99 alone is about 9.5 billion. That comes from the release client's own EXP formula. Victoria Island has no strong map past the 60s. Wild Kargo (Lv 62), Tauromacis (Lv 70) and Taurospear (Lv 75) all respawn on timers, so they train slowly.

      • Stay on the best maps you already use: the subway Wraith maps for the mage, Forest of Evil II or Sleepy Dungeon V for the thieves.
      • Same AP rule: thieves add DEX only for gear and the rest to LUK, the mage goes all INT.
      • Levels 71 to 100 give 90 more SP. Each build needs only 19 to finish its 2nd job skills. Nexon has not said whether the rest carries into 3rd job.
